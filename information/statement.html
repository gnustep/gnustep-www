Statement to users of Mac OS X

A lot of people waiting for Apple's Mac OSX system have been wondering what is the future direction of the project. We don't know either, but we are commited to producing a top-quality development environment in the image of OpenStep. We aren't there yet, and it may be rough going in the next few months before we can get something that is useful to people. As this is a volunteer effort we cannot promise any delivery dates. We do need more help, even if people just try to compile the stuff and tell us what problems they had. Stay tuned...
