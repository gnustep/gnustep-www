NSWorkspaceDidLaunchApplicationNotification

NSString* NSWorkspaceDidLaunchApplicationNotification;

This notification is sent by applications when they launch, the notification userInfo dictionary contains the following -

NSApplicationName
The name of the launched application. A string.
NSApplicationPath
The full path to the launched application. A string.
NSApplicationProcessIdentifier
The process identifier (pid) of the launched application.
NSApplicationProcessSerialNumberHigh
MacOS-X specific... not present in GNUstep.
NSApplicationProcessSerialNumberLow
MacOS-X specific... not present in GNUstep.
