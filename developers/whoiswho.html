Adam Fedor, fedor@gnu.org
GNUstep chief maintainer, DGS maintainer. Works on configuration, makefiles, and the xdps and xgps libraries.

Nicola Pero n.pero@mi.flashnet.it, nicola@brainstorm.co.uk,
