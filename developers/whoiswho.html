Developers and Projects

Adam Fedor (fedor at gnu dot org)
GNUstep chief maintainer. Works on configuration, makefiles, and the backend libraries. Updates to the website.

Richard Frith-MacDonald (richard at brainstorm dot co dot uk, rfm at gnu dot org)
Main developer for base library. Developer for gui, and backend libraries. Wrote Gorm (InterfaceBuilder clone). Wrote GNUstep-guile interface and testsuite. Anyone wishing to submit test cases, please talk to me!

Nicola Pero (n.pero at mi dot flashnet dot it, nicola at brainstorm dot co dot uk)
Main developer for make, gui. Wrote JIGS (the GNUstep Java/Objective-C bridge). Contributed to the GNUstep backend libraries, Objective-C runtime library, testsuite, and something to most other GNUstep core packages. Wrote tutorial documentation.

Fred Kiefer (FredKiefer at gmx dot de)
Working on GUI and Windows backend and other backend. Lots of work on text and fonts.

Gregory Casamento (greg_casamento at yahoo dot com)
Main developer for Gorm.

Pierre-Yves Rivaille (pyrivail at ens-lyon dot fr)
Major developer for Gorm.

Alexander Malmberg (alexander at malmberg dot org)
Main developer for gui and back. Wrote art backend, new text system. Lots of other contributions.

Mirko Viviani (mirko.viviani at rccr dot cremona dot it)
Developer on GUI library. Work on browsers, cells, windows, etc.

Philippe C.D. Robert (robert at siggraph dot org)
Lead developer of the GNU 3DKit. Wrote ProjectCenter and other GNUstep applications.

Manuel Guesdon (mguesdon at sbuilders dot com)
Lead developer of GNUstepWeb. Also maintains gnustep.org DNS, and mirrors

Andrew Lindesay
Responsible for the design of GNUstep icons.

Michael Hanni (mhanni at sprintmail dot com)
GNUstep hacker/cheerleader.
