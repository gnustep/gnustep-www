GNUstep DGS Developement

The GNU project is NO LONGER soliciting volunteers to work on Display Ghostscript. DGS is a very complicated system and we have not found anyone with sufficient knowledge to lead this project.

The Display Ghostscript System is needed on operating systems which do not have the Adobe Display PostScript System. Most commercial UNIX systems by Sun, HP, IBM, Digital come with Adobe's DPS as part of the X Window System. The Linux/GNU operating system, however, is one major exception.
